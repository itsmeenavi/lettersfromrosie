**Letters from You** is a forthcoming interactive book that blends physical storytelling with a digital community.

## The Concept

Hidden within the back cover of the physical book is a QR code. When scanned, it opens an interactive digital experience where readers can unlock a virtual wax-sealed envelope. Inside are letters written by readers around the world, mapped geographically.

### Themes Explored

- The ephemeral nature of digital communication
- The enduring warmth of handwritten letters
- Global interconnectedness through shared emotional experiences

*Release Date: Winter 2024*
