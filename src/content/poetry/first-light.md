---
title: "First Light at the Harbor"
date: "2023-10-14"
category: "Poetry"
desc: "A reflection on morning stillness and the quiet boats waiting for the tide."
---

The harbor wakes before the sun,  
A canvas painted slate and grey.  
The quiet boats, their resting done,  
Await the breaking of the day.  

Soft ripples lap against the pier,  
A gentle rhythm, slow and deep.  
The morning mist begins to clear,  
As seagulls stir from heavy sleep.  

I stand upon the wooden dock,  
And watch the horizon start to glow.  
The world forgets the ticking clock,  
Embracing what the light will show.
